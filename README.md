#### About

I run the commercial side of [Altegio](https://alteg.io/en/), a SaaS booking and business-management platform for beauty and wellness businesses, with teams and customers across Europe, Brazil and Kazakhstan.

I don't just ask for automation. I build it myself. I use AI coding tools to turn sales operations, coaching and analytics into systems that run every day.

#### What I'm building

- **Gigi**: an AI assistant for our commercial team that runs on my own server. It works inside Slack and connects to Notion and our CRM. It analyses sales calls, remembers context between conversations, and gives sales managers a daily coaching review.
- **Sales automation**: removing manual work from the sales process, from CRM routines to follow-ups and team reporting.
- **Sales analytics**: Power BI dashboards that show the funnel, team performance and growth across markets.
